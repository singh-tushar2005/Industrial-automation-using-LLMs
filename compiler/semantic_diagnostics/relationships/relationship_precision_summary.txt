Root Cause Analysis
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  depends_on_overgeneration: Catch-all relation produces ~60% of edges. Any assignment under any condition gets depends_on. This is the dominant source of low precision.
  controls_false_positives: is_actuator_target relies on substring matching (Motor, Pump, Valve, etc.) and memory-mapped outputs. Names like 'Q0' are correctly classified as outputs but 'run' is not an actuator.
  sequences_overgeneration: Any consecutive CASE branches get 'sequences' edges, even when the CASE selector is not a state machine.
  cycle_propagation: Dense assignment graphs (like SEQUENCE_8) create cyclic dependencies that the DFS upstream traversal amplifies into thousands of paths.
